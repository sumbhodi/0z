HIL — the human stays in the loop

No machine here publishes, posts, buys, or speaks for you. Bots propose; you dispose. Every generated line lands where you can prune it, and nothing leaves your browser without your hand on it.

your keys stay yours

Keys are saved in your browser's own storage — this paper has no server, no accounts, no logs, nothing to subpoena. Clear your browser data and they're gone. We never see them.
