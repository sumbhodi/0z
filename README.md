## how a bot works here

Chat names the cards it needs. A planner writes the steps, one per line. A runner fires the tools. A checker reads the surface back and says what actually landed. You watch every step, and anything that leaves the device waits for your green press. Small models are welcome: the plan is a list, the tools carry their own instructions, and a wrong step is a wall, not a lie.

## keys

Anthropic · Gemini · Groq (free tier) · OpenAI · xAI · Mistral · DeepSeek · OpenRouter · Hugging Face · a local engine on `127.0.0.1`. Keys live in your browser's storage and never touch a server of ours.

## accessibility

It is the product, not a courtesy: every control named, large targets, keyboard-walkable, a steadier-targets mode for shaky-hand days, bigger-everything at one press, a clinical high-contrast skin.
