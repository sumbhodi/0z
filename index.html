A desk of AI workspaces that runs in your browser. Bring your own key. Nothing leaves your device.

  • 🧠 0zevery card and every bot on one desk — the tutor, the nurse, the coder, the newsroom, the arcade, the clock.
  • 🦴 desk n dogthe desk, the dog, and the chart he wakes up reading.
  • ⚕️ hello nursea clipboard that remembers. sleep, food and movement in plain words, never a diagnosis.
  • 🎓 the tutorasks before it answers. for anyone who was told once that they were not a maths person.
  • 🫙 the titheall three at once — the dog, the nurse and the tutor in one desk.

Vanilla JavaScript, no build step, no server: your key calls the provider directly and your notes stay in this browser. Free tickets exist (Groq, Gemini). Also live at 0zhub.com. Code on GitHub — Apache 2.0, art reserved. Built by hand with Claude.
